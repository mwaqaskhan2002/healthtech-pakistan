history_edu THE ORIGIN STORY

Born at the Crossroads of Clinical Need and Software Engineering

In late 2020, three hospital ward rounds across Karachi, Lahore, and Rawalpindi exposed the same critical vulnerability: patients carrying handwritten files across provinces, lost historical records leading to duplicate invasive procedures, and zero interoperability between clinical departments.

A small cluster of frontline doctors, biomedical engineers, and health policymakers began meeting on weekends to design open-standard prototypes. What began as an informal working group evolved into Pakistan’s central non- partisan ecosystem for digital health transformation, providing technical roadmaps for nationwide electronic health records and rural telemedicine.
